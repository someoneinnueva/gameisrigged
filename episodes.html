EP01
Education & Policy

The School Arms Race

No Child Left Behind was supposed to close the gap between rich and poor schools. Instead it taught everyone how to game the test: teachers erased wrong answers, schools drowned their budgets in test prep, and the kids who needed the most help usually got the least. This episode is about what happens when a number becomes the goal instead of just a way to measure one.

EP02
Big Tech

The Subscription Trap

Free trials that quietly start charging you. Cancel buttons hidden behind four or five extra screens. None of this happens by accident: companies have figured out that making you leave is a lot more profitable than making you want to stay. This one's about how that trick actually works, why it gets basically everyone at least once, and why it's not going away anytime soon.

EP03
Personal Finance

Credit Card Rewards

Swiping a rewards card feels like getting free money, but someone's paying for those points, and it isn't the credit card company. We follow the fee all the way back from your purchase to find out who's really funding your airline miles, why people who can least afford it end up subsidizing everyone else's cash back, and why the miles you've been saving up could be worth half as much by tomorrow.

Carbon Credits

with Stephen Lezak

In this episode of The Game Is Rigged, we break down the hidden economics, game theory, and flawed incentives of the global carbon offset market. Joined by Stephen Lezak, a researcher at Oxford and Berkeley specializing in environmental justice and carbon finance, we look under the hood of an unregulated system where practically no one is incentivized to tell the truth.
