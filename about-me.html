Background I'm a current student at the Nueva School. I volunteer at Sunnybrae Elementary School and am interested in economics and its real-world applications.
Interests Economics, Game Theory, Public Policy, Mathematics, Education, Soccer

“Hey, I'm Anay.”

I live in the Bay Area and started this show after noticing how often the systems around us reward the wrong thing. Constantly, the incentives just feel a bit broken. On this show, we're just taking a step back to look at those hidden rules and talk about how we can improve these incentives for ourselves, and create a better future for the everyday citizen.
